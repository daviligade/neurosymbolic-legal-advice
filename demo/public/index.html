What am I seeing?

Client control and adviser accountability. Clients and firms have different knowledge, interests and responsibilities. This demo makes both sides' positions visible: what you authorised, why the adviser recommended a product, and what remains unanswered. The record governs later actions, including reconsidering advice when its support changes. Recording your choices does not transfer the firm's responsibilities to you.

A fictional investment example. You act as the client. A sustainability minimum is the lowest disclosed share of sustainable investments you will accept: 30% means at least 30%. Four products are checked. When C and D both qualify, you choose whether lower cost or a higher sustainable share matters more.

LIBRA and the AI have different roles. LIBRA tracks who supports each claim, relevant alternatives, reasons and explanations still owed. Separate rules require confirmation and check the advice. Four guided examples use prepared messages; “Try your own messages” uses a Groq-hosted model to map your words to proposed actions. Replies come from checked templates.

This research demo illustrates technical controls with hypothetical products; it provides no personal investment advice. Better understanding, fairer outcomes, reduced manipulation and reduced legal risk are goals for further evaluation. The study does not establish these outcomes or complete legal compliance. The model can misunderstand your message: review each proposal before confirming it.

Inspect the research details

The tracker records who supports each claim, its supporting reasons and questions still awaiting an answer. The alternative tracker was built for this study to implement the same dialogue rules using ordinary records. Both trackers share advice and confirmation checks, so these examples can produce identical replies.

Changing the tracker replays this guided interaction with the alternative implementation. The live conversation uses LIBRA. The demo is not a new comparative performance evaluation.

The guided examples make no model calls. Live messages are interpreted by Groq; your separate confirmations are handled locally. Conversation records are kept in this browser’s memory and reconstructed on the server. Recent requests may be cached briefly in server memory to prevent duplicate calls; no conversation database is used. Hosting and model providers may process request metadata.
